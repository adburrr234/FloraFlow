FloraFlow Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • Version 1.2

Data Protection Compliant
1. Overview & Scope

FloraFlow ("we", "our", or "the System") is committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and security of all personal, operational, and customer data processed through our Nursery Management System. This Privacy Policy details our practices concerning data collection, storage, operational utilization, and safeguarding measures.

This policy applies to all administrators, staff members, store associates, and suppliers interacting with the FloraFlow portal across web and mobile interfaces.

2. Information We Collect
Category Data Elements Purpose
Staff Information Full name, username, encrypted password, role, designation, phone, email, hire date, salary. Authentication, access control, shift tracking, and payroll administration.
Inventory Data Plant species, accessories, categories, pricing, stock levels, and care instructions. Catalog management, automated reordering, and POS lookups.
Sales & Transactions Sale timestamps, line items, quantities, payment modes, and cashier IDs. Billing, revenue reporting, tax records, and purchase history.
Operational Logs Attendance clock-ins, assigned tasks, plant care schedules, and system alerts. Workforce management, nursery health monitoring, and auditability.
3. Data Security & Storage Architecture

FloraFlow applies robust multi-layer cybersecurity standards to safeguard system records:

  • Database Encryption: All database interactions with our distributed TiDB cloud cluster are enforced through TLS/SSL encrypted channels using dedicated Certificate Authority (CA) validation.
  • Access Controls: Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) separates staff workflows from administrative features, restricting sensitive operations like salary reviews and database credentials.
  • Session Integrity: Web sessions utilize cryptographically signed cookies with HTTP-only flags to prevent session hijacking and cross-site scripting vulnerabilities.
  • Secret Management: Environment configurations and connection keys are strictly quarantined in secured environment variables.
4. Data Retention & User Rights

Staff members retain the right to review their registered profile details, request credential updates, and verify assigned operational tasks. Administrative records, including sales ledgers and inventory procurement histories, are retained in accordance with commercial nursery accounting regulations.

Quick Summary
  • Zero third-party selling of nursery operational or staff data.
  • End-to-end SSL encryption on all database queries and backups.
  • Strict role separation between Store Associates and Admin managers.
  • Transparent attendance, task tracking, and inventory logs.
